My apology

Please read this slowly

Babe, I’m so sorry for what happened between us. It started that morning when I called you to say I was going to work, and you didn’t respond to me properly. You just said, “OK, bye,” and it really hurt me. I could have just sent you a text saying I was leaving, but I wanted to tell you every day because I get really excited about going to work and wanted to share that with you. Because of that, I got hurt and texted you, saying it was wrong of you to talk to me like that.

After that, you got mad at me and ignored me while you were sitting and waiting for that man. I texted you asking you to talk to me while you waited, and you replied, “No, I don’t want to ruin my mood.” You always say that I ruin your mood, your work, and everything, which is so unfair. Even when you ruin my mood, I never say things like that to you, but you always do.

Two or three hours later, I asked if you were still mad at me, but you started yelling instead of answering. If you had just answered my question, you could have gone back to your work, scrolled TikTok, or done whatever you wanted. You kept yelling at me without answering, which made me mad too. I grew cold, and harsh words and curses were exchanged, which really hurt me.

The next day, I still called you and asked to make up and let go of the anger, but you didn’t care. I tried again the next day, and you still didn’t care, and then you called me without using curse words, so I decided to go to sleep. But while I was on TikTok, the filter malfunctioned, and I saw two terrifying faces in the dark. You know how scared I am of ghosts, and I had nobody else to talk to. I asked you to just stay on call with me, even if we weren’t talking, but you completely ignored me.

Feeling helpless, I said, “Screw it, I’m blocking you,” and I blocked you on every social media platform. I cried and had to call my mother at 1:00 AM just to stay on the phone with me so I could fall asleep because I was so terrified.

Despite all of that pain—and even though part of me thought we were done—I am still trying. I am building this for you to show that I am improving just like you wanted. I’m hoping you will accept my apology and make things right. Please, I love you.
